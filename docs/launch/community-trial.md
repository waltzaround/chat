# Community trial invitation

Send individually to an admin whose community you know, adjusting the opening to their actual group. Do not bulk-post it.

Hi [name] — I’m building Chat, an open-source Discord-style app that runs in your own Cloudflare account. I thought it might suit [specific project or meetup] because [specific reason].

Would you be interested in trying it for one project channel or a single meetup? I can help with deployment and collect your feedback on setup and day-to-day use.

Here’s a no-signup text-chat preview and a recording of the real app: https://chat.walt.online/#demo
Source: https://github.com/waltzaround/chat

It’s early: the browser app is the easiest starting point, mobile clients require source builds, and voice needs separate configuration. A small trial would help me learn what needs work before you rely on it for a larger group.

## Follow-up for an agreed trial

- Help the admin deploy in their own account; let them enter credentials themselves.
- Choose a small project or scheduled event and invite its participants.
- Ask where setup or inviting members became confusing.
- After the trial, ask whether members returned and what would keep the group using it.
